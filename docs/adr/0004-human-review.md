# ADR 0004: Require human review and manual publication

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Generated professional content can contain unsupported claims, awkward voice, licensing issues, or account-impacting mistakes.

## Decision

All drafts enter a review workflow. Approval is explicit and gated by references, claim status, channel validation, and diagram validation. Publication remains manual in v1.

## Consequences

- Lower account and reputation risk.
- The user retains authorship and voice.
- No social-network credentials or publishing permissions are needed for the MVP.
- Automated publishing may be added only as a separate, opt-in adapter and threat-model change.
